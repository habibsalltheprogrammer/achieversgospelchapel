Testimonies of His Grace

Every testimony is a monument to God's faithfulness. Read what the Lord has done and be encouraged in your own walk of faith.

✝️ Salvation
I had been living in darkness for years — addiction, broken relationships, no hope. A friend invited me to Achievers Gospel Chapel one Sunday and I sat at the back, arms crossed, ready to leave early. But when Pastor Samuels began to preach, I felt something I had never felt before. I gave my life to Christ that day and have not looked back since. My family is restored, I am free from addiction, and I have found my purpose in God.

🏥 Healing
I was diagnosed with a tumour and given only months to live. My family was devastated. But the intercessory team at Achievers Gospel Chapel stood with us in prayer, fasting, and faith. I went back to the hospital after six weeks of intensive prayer and the doctors could not find the tumour. They ran multiple tests, all confirming — it was gone! God completely healed me and I am standing here today as a living testimony of His miracle power.
