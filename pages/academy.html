About Achievers Royal Academy

Achievers Royal Academy is the educational arm of Achievers Gospel Chapel, established to provide quality, Christ-centred education to children in Freetown and the surrounding community. We combine strong academics with godly character formation, preparing every child to excel in life and in faith.

✝️ Christian Values

Biblical teaching and godly character are woven into everyday learning, not just Bible class.

👩🏾‍🏫 Qualified Teachers

A caring, experienced teaching staff committed to each child's academic and spiritual growth.

🛡 Safe Environment

A secure, nurturing campus where every child is known, valued, and encouraged to thrive.

📚 Strong Academic Foundation

A rigorous curriculum designed to prepare students for future academic success and lifelong achievement.
