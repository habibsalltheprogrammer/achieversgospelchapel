Medical Outreach

Our flagship community health programme provides free medical care, health screenings, and wellness education to underserved communities in Freetown. Run in partnership with medical professionals who are members of the church and community volunteers.

  • General Consultations
  • Malaria Testing
  • Antenatal Care
  • Prayer Ministry

Evangelism & Church Planting

Our evangelism team regularly takes the gospel to the streets, markets, hospitals, prisons, and schools of Freetown. We believe every person deserves to hear the good news of Jesus Christ.

  • Street Evangelism
  • Prison Ministry
  • Hospital Visits
  • School Outreach

Charity & Welfare

Reaching the vulnerable — widows, orphans, the elderly, and the poor — with practical love and support. We believe faith without works is dead, and we put our faith into action through tangible acts of generosity.

  • Food Distributions
  • Widow Support
  • School Supplies
  • Orphan Care
