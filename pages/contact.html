Plan Your First Visit

We would love to welcome you. Here's everything you need to know before you come.

📅 When Do We Meet?

Sunday Worship at 9:00 AM. Bible Study on Wednesdays at 6:00 PM. Prayer on Fridays at 6:00 AM. Youth on Saturdays at 3:00 PM.

👔 What Should I Wear?

Come as you are! We are a welcoming community. Smart-casual attire is typical for Sunday services, but there is no strict dress code.

👶 What About My Children?

We have an active Children's Ministry during Sunday services. Your children will be safe, loved, and engaged in age-appropriate teaching.

🤝 What Can I Expect?

Warm welcome, anointed worship, powerful Bible teaching, and a community that genuinely cares. You will feel at home from your very first visit.

🚗 How Do I Get There?

We are located at 27 Davies Street, Kissy Shell Old Road, Freetown. Easily accessible by road and public transport. Limited parking available on site.

❓ Have More Questions?

Our team is happy to answer any questions you may have. Call us on +232 76 618024 or use the contact form above. We look forward to meeting you!
