About Achievers Gospel Chapel

Rooted in faith, growing in love, and reaching out with the gospel of Jesus Christ to all of Sierra Leone and beyond.

Our Story

Church History

From humble beginnings to a vibrant gospel community — this is the story of God's faithfulness.

The Beginning
3rd July 2016 — The Beginning

Achievers Gospel Chapel was founded on 3rd July 2016 by Rev. Eustace Samuels and his wife, driven by a burning passion to reach the lost and build a community of believers in Freetown, Sierra Leone. Starting with a small gathering of faithful members, the church planted its roots at 27 Davies Street, Kissy Shell Old Road.

Growth
2018–2020 — Growing Strong

As the ministry grew, new departments and programmes were established. The Chosen Generation Youth Department was formed, bringing young people together through dance, music, drama, and poetry. The Medical Outreach programme began touching lives in the surrounding community.

Ministry Expansion
2022–2026 — A Decade of Impact

Intimacy Worship nights became a regular feature, drawing hundreds to encounter God in deep, powerful worship. Ecclesiastes was founded by Vince The Poet, creating a unique space where art, creativity, and the gospel intersect.

Today

Achievers Gospel Chapel continues to grow as a vibrant, multi-generational church. With over 15 active departments, regular outreach activities, and a growing online presence, the ministry is fulfilling its mission to liberate souls, equip the saints, and stand steadfast in the faith.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

Our beliefs are grounded in the Holy Scriptures — the infallible Word of God.

📖 The Holy Bible

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God — the supreme standard for faith and conduct.

✝️ The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son (Jesus Christ), and Holy Spirit — co-equal and co-eternal.

🌟 Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, ascension, and imminent return.

🕊 The Holy Spirit

We believe in the person and work of the Holy Spirit — His baptism, gifts, and fruit operative in believers today for ministry and holy living.

💫 Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone. Repentance and new birth are necessary for all who are saved.

🙏 Healing

We believe in divine healing as provided for in the atonement of Christ and available to believers through prayer and faith.

⛪ The Church

We believe in the universal Church as the body of Christ, and in the local church as the expression of God's kingdom on earth.

🌅 Eternal Life

We believe in the resurrection of both the saved and the lost — the saved to eternal life, the lost to eternal separation from God.

Organisation

Church Leadership Structure

  • 👑

    Senior Pastors

    Rev. Eustace Samuels & Rev. Mrs. Eustace Samuels — overall spiritual oversight and direction of the ministry.

  • 🏛

    Board of Elders

    Seasoned spiritual leaders who provide counsel, accountability, and governance to the church.

  • ✝️

    Ministers

    Ordained and licensed ministers who assist in preaching, teaching, pastoral care, and ministry functions.

  • 🔥

    Department Heads

    Leaders of the various auxiliary and functionary departments who coordinate and oversee departmental activities.

  • 🌱

    Cell / Home Group Leaders

    Facilitators who lead small groups for fellowship, discipleship, and community building.

  • 👥

    General Members

    The heart of the church — every member is valued, gifted, and called to serve in the body of Christ.
