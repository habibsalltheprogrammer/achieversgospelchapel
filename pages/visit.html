Questions?

Visitor FAQs

Common questions from first-time visitors. Don't see your question? ContactUs directly!

Do I need to be a Christian to attend? +

Absolutely not! Achievers Gospel Chapel welcomes everyone — believers and seekers alike. Whether you have faith, have questions, or are simply curious, you are welcome to come and experience church with us. We are a non-judgmental community.

What should I wear? +

Come as you are! There is no formal dress code. Most members dress smart-casually for Sunday services. What matters most to us is that you are comfortable and present. God looks at the heart, not the wardrobe.

How long is the Sunday service? +

Our Sunday worship service typically runs from 9:00 AM to approximately 11:30 AM — about two and a half hours. This includes praise and worship, announcements, the sermon, prayer ministry, and an offering. Special services and programmes may run longer.

Is there parking available? +

Yes, we have on-site parking available at the church premises on Davies Street. For larger events and special services, additional street parking is available on nearby roads. We recommend arriving early on busy Sundays and special occasions.

Do I have to give money? +

There is no obligation for first-time visitors to give. An offering is collected during service as an act of worship for church members. We will never pressure anyone to give, and there is absolutely no charge to attend any of our services or programmes.

Can I become a member? +

Yes! We have a structured membership process that includes an orientation class, meeting with the pastoral team, and a formal membership commitment. Membership gives you full participation rights in the church community and access to all ministry opportunities. ContactUs to start the process.

What if I need prayer or pastoral care? +

We have a trained pastoral team and intercessory ministry available to pray with you. You can submit a prayer request online, approach one of our ministers after service, or contact the church office to schedule a pastoral appointment. All conversations are treated with the utmost confidentiality.

Are your services available online? +

We live stream our Sunday services on our social media platforms. Follow us on Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram for live streams and recorded services. You can also access sermon recordings on our Sermons page on this website.
