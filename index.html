A Ministry That Transforms Lives

Achievers Gospel Chapel is a ministry that preaches the gospel of Jesus Christ to all — healing the broken hearted, delivering the captives, recovering of sight to the blind, and setting at liberty those that are bruised.

We are a community rooted in love, prayer, and the Word of God. Located in the heart of Freetown, Sierra Leone, we welcome all who are searching for a spiritual home.

🎯 Our Mission

Liberating Souls, Equipping The Saints And Standing Steadfast In The Faith

🌟 Our Vision

Building Lives And Shaping Destinies

💎 Core Values

Faith • Love • Prayer • Unity • Service • Excellence • Integrity • Diligience
